cyto7 v6 — hand-painted release (canonical): cingulate rings completed
======================================================================
Source: pial.{lh,rh}.cyto7.v5.annot -> pial.{lh,rh}.cyto7.v6.annot
cyto7 stays 7 labels. v6 is the RELEASED / CANONICAL map.

Origin
------
Hand-painted by Ricardo Salvador (RS) in the painting tool, starting from v5. RS
closed the cingulate DYSGRANULAR outer ring and completed the AGRANULAR inner ring,
mostly by painting genuinely-unpainted cingulate cortex (cyto7-unknown vertices that
lie on real cortex per FreeSurfer ?h.cortex.label) as dysgranular / agranular, plus a
minor right-hemisphere allocortex extension. Isocortex was not touched. The true
medial wall (outside ?h.cortex.label) was never painted.

Per-vertex manual diff: v5_to_v6_changelog.csv (1,931 vertices: LH 844, RH 1087).
