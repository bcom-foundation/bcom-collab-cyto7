\caption{\textbf{Supplementary Table S8. Outcome table: the declared FDR families.} Every test in the declared FDR families, with its hypothesis status, statistical unit, null, FDR family, raw $p$, within-family $q$ and effect size. Status records whether a test was specified in advance, not whether its result is retained: the connectivity analysis of Annex~G was specified in advance but is reported as exploratory there, because it does not survive a spin null or a bundle-level model. An asterisk marks a test that survives the null in its Null column: at $q<0.05$ within its family where Benjamini--Hochberg correction was applied, and at $p<0.05$ where it was not. Families are never pooled. Tests reported without a $q$ had no FDR applied within their family and are marked accordingly; Table~S4 lists every family with its size, whether correction was applied and why. Rows whose status is \emph{descriptive} carry no null and no test and are never asterisked: the per-feature win fractions on the disagreement set are reported this way, because the vertices are not independent and the chance baseline for a win fraction is not 0.5 but the level of the relevant empirical null, which differs between comparisons. The aggregate win fractions are tested against their own null distributions and are asterisked. A few robustness analyses belong to a declared family and are listed here with the status \emph{robustness}; those that belong to no declared family are disclosed separately in Table~S9, whose caption states the correction policy applied to them and the one exception to it.}\label{tab:outcomes}\\
